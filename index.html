📖 How to Play

  1. Choose a target between 30 and 300.
  2. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty.
  3. You and the computer take turns choosing a number from 1 to 10.
  4. Each chosen number is added to the running total.
  5. The player who makes the total reach the target exactly wins!
  6. You cannot go above the target.
  7. When the remaining amount is less than 10, only valid numbers are available.

Good luck! 🍀
